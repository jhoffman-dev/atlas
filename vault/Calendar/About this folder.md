# Calendar

Notes in here are written by the **Milestones** source in `.atlas/sources`, from
`feeds/milestones.ics`. They are ordinary notes: edit one and the next refresh
keeps your text and rewrites only the fields the feed owns. Take a record out of
the feed and its note is marked `atlas_source_missing`, never deleted; put it back
and the next refresh takes the mark off.
