Pulled into Phase 13 rather than done on its own. A split is a change to how the
app is laid out, and doing it while the shell is already being rebuilt is far
cheaper than retrofitting it into a finished one — every pane-aware decision
(where the header goes, what a focused pane looks like, what the sidebar
highlights) is a decision Phase 13 is making anyway.

Two panes, side by side, each holding any note — a board beside the note you are
working on, a calendar beside a daily note, the same note twice. Which pane a
click lands in is the thing to get right: opening a link should replace the pane
you are in, not the other one.

Open question to settle while building: whether the split survives a restart.
It should, but that means remembering it somewhere, and the only honest place is
a per-viewer UI-state file rather than anything in the vault.

Settled: it survives, in local storage behind a port, beside the theme and the
sidebar's collapsed sections — and against the vault it belongs to, since the
paths are relative to one and restoring a split into another vault would open
two panes on notes that are not there.
