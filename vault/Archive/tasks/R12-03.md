`parseIcs` only treats `BEGIN`/`END` as special for `VEVENT`, so a nested `VALARM` — which every Google and Apple export carries — has its `DESCRIPTION` overwrite the event's, and leaves `begin`, `action`, `trigger` and `end` on the note. Track nesting depth and skip to the matching `END:`.
