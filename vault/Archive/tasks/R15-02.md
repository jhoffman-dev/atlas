From the code-reviewer pass on `apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/api/*` after A15-01.

1. **Major.** `adversarial_tests.rs:98-104` (`a_client_that_never_reads_…`) can hang forever: the server's RST lands in a zero window and is dropped, `read_to_end` waits on real I/O with no timer. Wrap reads/writes in a paused-clock `timeout`.
2. **Major, design.** 32 token-less connections that pipeline refused requests and never read hold every slot for 120 s. Send `Connection: close` on every host-side refusal (400/401/403/404/413) so only a token holder can keep a connection; say in ADR-0016 that availability against local processes is out of scope.
3. **Minor.** Off→on in quick succession sees Atlas's own old listener as a squatter, takes a new port and rotates the token (43/50 in a probe). Wait for the accept task to finish on stop, or recognise our own last port.
4. **Minor.** Requests before the webview's router is listening (startup, reload) wait 30 s → 504. Fail fast until the router says it is ready, or document it.
5. **Minor.** `admit` takes 4 params; `accept_until_stopped` ~40 lines; nits: `all()` name, `fn control` beside `mod control`, no dir fsync after rename.
