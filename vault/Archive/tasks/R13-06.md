A donut with counts [1000, 1] gives the second slice 0.0063 rad; subtracting a 0.02 pad hands `wedge()` a start angle past its end, and the renderer draws the mirrored minor arc — a sliver on the wrong side of the seam. Clamp the pad to the sweep, or drop the wedge when the sweep is smaller than the pad.
