Tree rows are `tabIndex={-1}` with no roving tabindex, so arrow keys do not move
between them and never have — this predates the five-section sidebar rather than
being caused by it. Arrow keys also do not sweep across section boundaries,
which is the accepted cost of five disclosures instead of one `role="tree"`.

What did improve: the favourite stars are real buttons, and they are the first
keyboard route into a tree row this app has had.

Worth doing properly — roving tabindex within User space, Home/End, and
type-ahead — but it is the same class of work as the headless-primitives phase,
and a tree is one of the things no library gives you. Sequence it accordingly.
