`capture-palette.tsx` and `search-palette.tsx` handle Enter themselves and do not wait out a composition, so a Japanese, Chinese or Korean user pressing Enter to confirm a character creates a task, or opens a note, from half-typed text. Base UI already waits for Escape; the palettes' own Enter does not. Check `isComposing` **and** WebKit's `keyCode === 229` after `compositionend`, which is what the macOS webview sends. Tests: `packages/ui/src/palette-ime.test.tsx` (3 failing, 2 passing controls).
