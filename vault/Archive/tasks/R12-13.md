`Date.parse('2026-02-30')` rolls over, so a task written `2026-02-30` is drawn on `2026-03-02` and `2026-02-29` in a non-leap year lands on `2026-03-01`. `2026-13-45` is correctly refused, so the module disagrees with itself: an impossible month is refused and an impossible day is guessed at. `parseIcs` will hand it `2026-02-30` quite happily.
