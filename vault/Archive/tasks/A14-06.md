- A timeline bar at `9999-12-29..9999-12-31` moved +1 day shrinks: the end cannot be written, so it stays, while the start moves. Keep the length or do not move.
- `spokenDate('0024-01-01')` names 1924's weekday: `Date.UTC` maps years 0–99 to 1900+. Use `setUTCFullYear`.
- `donutArcs([MAX_VALUE, MAX_VALUE])` overflows its sum to Infinity and draws nothing.
- `linePath` over 200,000 values with no `max` overflows the stack on `Math.max(...values)`; `dashboard-view.tsx` does the same spread.
