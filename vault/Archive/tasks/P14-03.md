Board, calendar and timeline onto dnd-kit.

They use native HTML5 drag today, which has **no keyboard path at all** — a live accessibility hole that shipped in Phase 13 knowingly. dnd-kit brings keyboard and touch with it.

The e2e specs drive drag through synthetic DragEvents with clientX (see `e2e/timeline.spec.ts`). Those will need rewriting against the new sensors — expect that, and add a keyboard-only drag test, which is the thing that could not be written before.
