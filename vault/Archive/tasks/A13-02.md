`donutArcs({ values: [1, 499], radius: 56, thickness: 22, padAngle: 0.02 })` — the dashboard's own numbers. The slice is 0.0126 rad wide and the pad is 0.02, so the wedge is drawn from a start angle past its end and the renderer paints the mirrored major arc: 6.28 rad of colour where the slice owns 0.0126.

A count of zero does the same, painting a pad-wide sliver for a category with nothing in it. Clamp the pad to the sweep, and paint nothing when the slice is narrower than its gap. Supersedes R13-06, which found the same root cause with one case.
