`openInPane` is composed at the call site as `openInFocused(focusPane(was, pane), path)`, and `focusPane` on a pane that does not exist is a no-op — so the open lands in the focused pane instead of being refused. A link click queued in pane 1 that arrives after pane 1 closed replaces the note you are actually reading.

The deeper point: there is no total `openInPane` in the domain, so every call site composes the rule itself and inherits the same hole.
