type: task

Found while building Phase 21; both predate it (ADR-0003/0004).

- Any email typed into a note saves as `julie\@example.com` once its paragraph is edited — the markdown writer escapes `@`. Needs a serializer handler so an `@` in text stays as written.
- A `[[link]]` (or `@` mention) picked at the very end of a paragraph, with nothing typed after it, saves as `[[Name]]&#x20;` — the trailing space the picker inserts is encoded. Drop or keep the space without the entity.

Round-trip corpus cases for both; check nothing else in the corpus changes.
