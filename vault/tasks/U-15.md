James, 2026-09-26: "Add the thumbnails that generate from the page as a property that can be add to any object."

Read as: a Thumbnail property kind available in the type editor and "Add a property"; Atlas pictures the note's own page (for artifacts, the saved copy, as today) and uses it on gallery cards. Generated pictures are derived, so they live in the cache; a picture the person picks is stored as a value.

Done: `kind: thumbnail` is in the type editor's kinds and in "Add a property"
(on one note it starts as `auto` and is known again by the key `thumbnail`).
Empty or `auto` means Atlas pictures the note's own page — title and body as
the app reads it, images written in, under a policy that runs and fetches
nothing — through the same host snapshot as artifacts, and keeps the picture
in `.atlas-cache/thumbnails/<hash of path>.png`, never in the vault. It is
made again when the note is newer than it, once the note has been quiet for
4 seconds and no pane holds unsaved edits to it. A path is a picture chosen
(Choose image… copies it into attachments like an embedded image); `false`
is cleared and stays so until Regenerate. A gallery of a type with one is
fronted by it, 16:10; a cleared one falls back to the note's `cover` or first
image. Artifacts' `cover` is declared `kind: thumbnail` too, but keeps its
own rule: an artifact's page is its saved copy. Board and list cards do not
show the picture (left out, not needed yet).
