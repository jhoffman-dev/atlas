Switching rewrites only that block.

Built: a link's "…" (under the pointer), a right-click, or Shift+F10 opens
Open note / Show as bookmark; a card's "…" offers Show as link. In the `[[`
list, Shift+Enter (or Shift-click) puts the link in as a bookmark. Only the
link's paragraph is rewritten; a link in a list, quote or table is not
offered a card. Awaiting the code-review and adversarial passes.
